# Overview

**Chunk loaders for survival servers:** one block, and your farm keeps running - even when you are offline. With a chunk budget everyone spreads over their loaders, invitations for friends, lag protection and a map in the browser (with MRadmin).

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| Server | Paper 26.3 (or a fork like Purpur), Java 25 |
| Requires | nothing - LuckPerms is recommended, MRadmin optional (for the web interface and the map) |
| Languages | German, English - every player automatically in the language of their Minecraft client |
| Status | stable (1.x) |

## What can MRchunks do?
- **Chunk loader blocks:** once placed, the area around them stays loaded permanently - in all dimensions, even when the player is offline. → [Using chunk loaders](chunkloader.md)
- **Free choice of size:** every player has a **chunk budget** (default 147 chunks = three 7×7 loaders) and sets the size of every loader themselves - also as a rectangle, e.g. 3×15 for a long farm. → [Size and chunk budget](groesse.md)
- **Invite friends:** farm partners may switch your loaders on and off. **Alt accounts** share loaders and budget. → [Friends & alt accounts](mitspieler.md)
- **Dummy players** (optional): an artificial player on the loader, so mobs stay active and farms run as if a real player stood next to them. → [Dummy players](dummy.md)
- **Indestructible:** no mining, no explosions, pistons, withers or endermen.
- **Lag protection:** loaders that slow the server down are detected and locked. When the server is under load, the staged shutdown temporarily turns off the most expensive loaders. → [Lag protection](lag-schutz.md), [Shutdown & pauses](abschaltung.md)
- **Map** with real terrain on MRadmin's player page. → [Map](karte.md)
- **Menus instead of commands** for players, an admin menu for the team, **Discord logs** and **update check**.
