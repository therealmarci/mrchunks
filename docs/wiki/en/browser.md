# In the browser (with MRadmin)

If MRadmin is installed on the server and the player page is switched on, `/web` in game gives you a link to your own page.

## Section chunk loaders
- At the top you see your **budget** and warnings (e.g. lag lock).
- Below, every loader is a card with status, size and load - with a button to switch it on and off.
- A click on a card shows the limits of the lag protection (warn and lock) and the buttons to rename, for the dummy and to delete. At the very bottom the small map **Surroundings** shows the loader with the terrain around it, "Open on the map" jumps to the big map.
- Below that you invite players to all loaders and see who is invited. Invitations for a single loader are in its card.
- Only the **size** is changed in game.

## Tab map
On the tab **Map** of the player page your own chunk loaders and the ones you are invited to are shown as areas in the color of their state (active green, off gray, locked red, conspicuous or disabled due to lag yellow). A click shows state and size, a **right click** switches the loader on or off. Under **Other chunk loaders** are the loaders of all other players (name, owner, size).

The team decides which of this exists on your server - see [Map (via MRadmin)](karte.md).
