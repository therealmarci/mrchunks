# Friends & alt accounts

## Invite friends
Invite farm partners so they can **switch your loaders on and off**, even when you are offline:
- **One loader:** the loader's menu → **Members** → **Invite a player**.
- **All loaders** (also future ones): `/chunkloader` → **Members for all loaders**.
- Choose the duration with the clock (1 day, 7 days, 30 days or permanent), then click a head. For offline players there is **Enter a name**.
- Invited players find the loaders under **Shared with me**. Only you may rename, delete and invite.
- Invitations do not count towards anyone's limit. If an invited player switches a loader on, it costs **your** budget.

## Link alt accounts
If you have an alt account (e.g. for AFK farming), link it to your main account. After that both behave like one player:
- Both see and manage **the same loaders**. What the alt account places belongs to the main account.
- There is **one shared budget** - the one of the main account.
- **Link:** `/chunkloader` → **Linked accounts** → **Link an account**. The other account has to be online and agree.
- Either of the two can **unlink** any time in the same menu. The loaders stay with the main account.
- By default one alt account is allowed. A ban applies to all linked accounts.

> [!NOTE]
> **For the team:** admins link and unlink accounts also without consent - with `/mrchunks link <main> <alt>` or `/mrchunks unlink <player>` or in the MRadmin web interface on the player page (`mrchunks.admin.link`). How many alt accounts are allowed: `linked-accounts.max-accounts`.
